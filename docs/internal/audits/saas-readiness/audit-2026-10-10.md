## 1. Verdict

**GOLD WITH CAVEATS (reconfirmed) — clean GOLD blocked by C6, C5, C4 and C3, all open-progressed.
C2 is closed.**

The strict ADR-001 fork-ready gate **passes**: zero P0; the six ADR-001 commands are green on Node
24 on the first run after `npm ci`; all six gated categories are at 83% or more; `LICENSE` and
`.env.example` are present. No exploitable security hole or cross-tenant exposure was found, and no
new P0 or P1.

All three fixes do what they were written to do, and each was reproduced:

- **U1 is closed.** A real `DatabaseError` holding an address and a hash was logged eight ways: as
  an argument, nested in metadata with `%j`, with `%o` and with no token, in an array, five levels
  deep with and without a token, and after a metadata object. No line carried the address, the
  hash or the SQL.
- **U2 is closed, and C2 with it.** The fork's production image, started with no `APP_NAME`, logs
  `service` as `acme-api`. The code-review agent, asked whether a fork is still branded as the
  upstream anywhere at runtime, found no place.
- **The cache-key route is closed.** A metric search for `victim@example.com` with a name filter,
  made twice, wrote `[CACHE] miss`, `stored` and `hit` lines naming `cursor:metrics:v3#00f1b20882ca`
  and no line with the address or the name. A rejected value, a rejected key name, a malformed
  body and an undecodable path, each holding the address, wrote no line with it.

S1, S2 and C1 are confirmed closed. Four caveats are not:

- **C6 (open-progressed, P2).** The security agent found every route earlier graders named closed,
  and kept the caveat open on two that nobody had named. A connection URL rejected at startup is
  printed whole, password included (V1). With `DB_LOGGING=true` and `LOG_LEVEL=debug`, SQL is
  logged with its values inlined (V2). Both reproduced.
- **C5 (open-progressed, P2).** The Sentry scrubber masks headers and the request body and leaves
  the URL, the query string, the user, breadcrumbs and exception values (V3). Reproduced. This is
  finding T7, which earlier runs carried beside a closed C5; this grader read it as C5 itself.
- **C4 (open-progressed, P3).** The lint rule that keeps `AppError` out of the domain layer misses
  the `@utils/` alias, has no negative case in the tests, and is absent for `src/shared/domain`
  (V4). Reproduced. No file in `src/` uses the gap today.
- **C3 (open-progressed, P3).** The error envelope is one shape everywhere. The spec documents a
  429 on 7 of 47 operations, and the app-wide limiter can answer 429 on any of them (V5). Counted.

Under the rule fixed before the evidence (kit D-02), one of the eight conditions open means the
verdict stays GOLD WITH CAVEATS; four are open. S9 and S10 are still open at P2.

## 3. Methodology

1. **Gates**, above, on Node 24 with real exit codes, after a clean `npm ci`.
2. **Grades** by the three agents, split as in the header, each caveat included. Labels:
   closed-confirmed / closed-regressed / open-unchanged / open-progressed.
3. **Fresh pass** by the same agents over what changed since `747d4b3`: twelve source files that
   log or cache, `app-name.ts`, two scripts, the analytics fixes and the lockfile.
4. **Fork dry-run** (§5): two fresh `--no-local` clones. On the first, bootstrap's output was read
   and nothing else was done. On the second, all five printed steps were executed, and its
   production image was built and started.
5. **Live reproductions** on the fork's server with throwaway services: S1, the registration race,
   the eight shapes of U1, the cache key, four rejected-request shapes, and every route the agents
   named for C6, C5, C4 and C3.
6. **Scans** from `audit-2026-05-01.md` Appendix B.
7. **Full 65-item re-grade** (§7). An item with no new evidence keeps its grade.
8. **Main-thread verification.** An agent's grade on a caveat stands unless a reproduction refutes
   it. Items not re-verified are marked _(agent)_.

The verdict rule was written before the evidence came in (kit D-02).

   refused (ADR-0036), or log statements without values.
4. **V4 / C4** (≤1h): add the `@utils/` spellings and the `src/shared/domain` block to the rule,
