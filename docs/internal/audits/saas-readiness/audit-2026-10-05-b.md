1. **Gates**, above, on Node 24 with real exit codes, after a clean `npm ci`.

   closed-confirmed / closed-regressed / open-unchanged / open-progressed.

   four scripts and `fork-smoke.yml`.

   before anything was changed, then all five printed steps were executed.
5. **Live reproductions** on the fork's server with throwaway services: S1, S8, T2, T1 (the race
   and a `%j` body), and the logging routes the security agent named.

8. **Main-thread verification.** An agent's grade on a caveat stands unless a reproduction refutes
   it. Items not re-verified are marked _(agent)_.
